Don't Forget the Lyrics
Don't Forget the Lyrics is a fast-paced musical trivia game where teams compete to test their memory, accuracy, and quick reflexes. Players navigate mystery boxes, song teasers, and sudden audio cuts where they must complete missing lyrics under tight time limits, deciding whether to keep mystery point values or pass them to another team.
Setup:
There will be 4 teams, each composed of 4 main players.
The game board features 16 numbered mystery boxes, each hiding a song track, a hint element, and a secret point value.
To determine which team will start first and the overall playing order, a Wheel of Names or randomizer will be used.
Each team will be given access to the same online buzzer system for fairness during stealing attempts.

Game Structure & Rounds:
The game consists of 3 main rounds.
Round 1: Following the turn order, each team picks 1 box from the 10 available mystery boxes (4 boxes played in total).
Round 2: Following the same turn order, each team picks another box from the remaining options (4 boxes played in total).
A total of 8 boxes will be opened and played across the entire game.

Turn Flow & Song Challenge:
On their turn, the active team picks any remaining unopened box on the board.
Element / Hint Reveal: After choosing a box, a relevant visual element, clue, icon, or category related to the song will be displayed on screen before the audio plays.
The song will then start playing for everyone in the voice channel.
At a random point during the track, the music will stop abruptly.
The 3-Second Rule: The active team must immediately start singing and complete the next missing lyrics within 3 seconds after the music cuts off.
If the active team correctly sings and completes the missing lyrics, they succeed and advance to the Mystery Points decision.
If the active team fails to sing, provides incorrect lyrics, or exceeds the 3-second time limit:
➤ The round opens for a Steal by the other three non-active teams.
➤ The first team to buzz in gets the chance to steal.
➤ The stealing team has 3 seconds to correctly complete the lyrics.
➤ If the steal is correct, they gain control of the box's mystery points.
➤ If the steal is incorrect, no points are awarded, and the mystery points for that box are forfeited.

The "Give or Take" Mystery Points System:
Whenever a team correctly completes the lyrics (active team or stealing team), they gain rights to the mystery points in that box.
Before the point value inside the box is revealed on screen, the team must choose one option:
TAKE: Claim the mystery points for their own team score.
GIVE: Pass the mystery points directly to a designated opposing team.
Once the choice ("Give" or "Take") is finalized, the Gamemaster will reveal the true point value of the box.
Mystery points can be positive (+50, +100, +200 pts), or negative (-50, -100, -200 pts).
Scoring & Winning:
Points are awarded, deducted, or modified based on the "Give or Take" decision made for each opened box.
Total team scores from both Round 1 and Round 2 are accumulated across the event.
After both rounds are completed (8 boxes opened total), the team with the highest overall score is declared the Don't Forget the Lyrics Champion.
Notes:
The Gamemaster will moderate the audio cuts, verify lyric accuracy, and manage scoring decisions.
Lyrics must be sung or spoken clearly and accurately according to the official song lyrics.
