# Host keyboard instructions

Use the keyboard to run the game. The host command panel has been removed.

## Start and title card

- Press **Space**, **Enter**, or click **Start** to show the title card.
- Wait five seconds for **Space to continue** to appear at the bottom.
- Press **Space** or **Enter**, or click the title card, to open the stage.

## Stage and turns

- Press **Space** on the stage to randomize the turn order. This does not run automatically.
- Use **Arrow keys** to move between available boxes.
- Press **Enter** to reveal a selected box hint.
- Press **Enter** or **Space** to open the song arena.

## Song arena and verdict

- Press **Space** to start or pause the song.
- After the clip cutoff, press **C** or **Space** for correct, or **X** / **W** for wrong.
- On the steal screen, use **Arrow keys** to select a team or No Steal, then press **Enter**.
- On Take It / Give It, use **Left / Right** (or **1 / 2**) and press **Enter**.
- In team selection, use **Arrow keys** and **Enter** to choose.
- Press **Enter** or **Space** to reveal points and advance through confirmation screens.
- Press **Escape** to back out where supported.
